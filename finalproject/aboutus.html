Key Considerations

When considering hypnotherapy, it is important to understand that it is a complementary approach, not a magic solution and requires active participation and commitment from the individual. Contrary to popular myth, the person remains aware and in control all the time and cannot be forced to act against his will. Hypnotherapy aims to put individuals in control of their though patterns and emotions, which often leads to a boost in self-esteem and self-confidence. It uses deep relaxation, focused attention, and openness to suggestion to help individuals reframe negative thought patterns.
